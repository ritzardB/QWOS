# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product roadmap for Quantum Workforce OS (QWOS).

The roadmap establishes the planned evolution of the platform from its initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to a mature enterprise Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

Rather than using fixed calendar dates, development is organized into milestone-based releases. A milestone is considered complete only after all acceptance criteria have been satisfied.

---

# Product Vision

Quantum Workforce OS will evolve into a unified Business Operating System that supports:

* Virtual Assistant Agencies
* HR Outsourcing Firms
* Recruitment Agencies
* Small and Medium Enterprises
* Professional Service Companies
* Multi-national Organizations

The platform will ultimately provide complete workforce, client, project, financial, and operational management through a secure cloud-based architecture.

---

# Development Strategy

Development follows five guiding principles:

* Build the foundation first.
* Deliver value incrementally.
* Validate features with real users.
* Maintain backward compatibility whenever practical.
* Prioritize quality over release speed.

# Release Management Principles

A release may proceed only when:

* Requirements are approved.
* UI designs are complete.
* Database changes are finalized.
* APIs are documented.
* Development is complete.
* Automated testing passes.
* Security review is complete.
* Documentation is updated.

No release should compromise platform stability or data integrity.

# Roadmap Summary

Quantum Workforce OS will evolve through disciplined, milestone-based development into a secure, scalable, and intelligent Business Operating System.

Every release will deliver measurable customer value while strengthening the long-term architecture of the platform.

The roadmap is intended to remain stable while allowing individual milestones and implementation details to evolve as customer feedback and business priorities change.
